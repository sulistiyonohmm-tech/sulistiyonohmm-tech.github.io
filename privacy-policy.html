Privacy Policy — BroFlow n8n

Last updated: September 11, 2026

What this application does

BroFlow n8n connects services that you choose so it can run the automations you configure.

Google data

When you connect Google services, the application uses the permissions you approve only to perform the requested automation, such as reading or writing a Google Sheet. We do not sell your Google data or use it for advertising.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and other credentials are stored in the n8n database in encrypted form. Access is restricted to the application service. You may disconnect a service at any time from n8n.

Data deletion

Disconnecting a credential removes it from the application. You may also request deletion of application data by contacting us.

Contact
